Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

CROW-G ("the App") is a personal automation tool operated by an individual developer (seonik.j@gmail.com) for their own use. This policy explains how the App handles Google user data.

1. Data the App accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the App may access only the data covered by the scopes you approve on the Google consent screen, which may include:

  • Basic profile information (name, email address)
  • Gmail messages and labels
  • Google Calendar events
  • Google Drive files

2. How the data is used

Google user data is used solely to provide the App's personal automation features to the account owner — for example, summarizing, labeling or organizing email, reading or creating calendar events, and reading or saving files. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. Storage and security

Data is processed on the owner's private computer. OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored locally and are not uploaded to third-party servers except where needed to perform a task the owner has requested. Reasonable measures are taken to protect this data from unauthorized access.

4. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. It is not transferred to others except as required by law.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to perform the requested task. You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored data, contact the address below.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
